📗 Cranford by Elizabeth Cleghorn Gaskell

~~~

the author is young
before the fire was lighted
who now sat in state

~~~

avant que le feu ne fût allumé
qui siège maintenant en majesté

🇯🇵 sakusha wa wakaku
hi ga tomosareru mae ni
ima wa kōrin ni suwaru
