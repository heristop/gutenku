📗 The Decameron of Giovanni Boccaccio by Giovanni Boccaccio

~~~

dreameth that a wolf
with certain words appeaseth
mangleth all his wife

~~~

🇫🇷 rêve qu’un loup
par certains mots apaise
déchire sa femme

🇯🇵 yume ni ookami ga
arutane kotoba de nagusamete
tsuma o hiki saku
