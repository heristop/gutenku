This haiku captures a quiet journey at daybreak, where the speaker and another person begin their path in the early morning light. The line "he perceived several trees" suggests a moment of gentle observation, as nature slowly comes into view. The final line, "Not a sound inside," creates a sense of stillness and inward emptiness, emphasizing silence both in the surrounding world and within the speaker’s mind or heart. Together, the poem evokes calm, solitude, and a reflective mood.
