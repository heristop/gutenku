This haiku creates a quiet, haunting image of a life shaped by darkness and emotional weight. The phrase "His life had been all shadows" suggests sorrow, hardship, or a lack of warmth and joy. Then, "turned aside their heads" gives the feeling of rejection, as if even the shadows themselves cannot fully face his suffering. The opening image, "are like a snowball," adds to the sense of something growing, gathering, and becoming heavier as it moves. Together, the lines suggest a life that has accumulated pain over time, making the poem feel solemn, reflective, and deeply human.
