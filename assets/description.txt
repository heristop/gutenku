This haiku suggests a moment of suspended thought and quiet tension. The phrase "would be in motion" implies energy, change, or movement that seems expected but not yet fully realized. "even late in the morning" adds a sense of time passing slowly, as if the day should already be underway, yet something remains unsettled. The final line, "The question now was," leaves the poem open-ended, emphasizing uncertainty and anticipation. Together, the lines create a reflective mood, where motion and stillness, clarity and doubt, exist side by side.
