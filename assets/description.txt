This haiku captures a moment of sudden danger and urgent despair. The image of a poisoned cup suggests betrayal or hidden harm, while the word "tottering" conveys weakness, shock, and instability as the person struggles toward her chamber. The final line, "No time for words now," heightens the tension by showing that speech, explanation, or comfort is no longer possible. Together, the lines create a tragic scene of silence, fear, and inevitability, where action has replaced language and fate is already unfolding.
