This haiku suggests a quiet moment of emotional acceptance and lingering desire. The phrase "having received them" implies that something or someone has been welcomed or accepted, while "even a little be friends" points to a modest but meaningful connection. Yet the final line, "which though it still yearned," introduces a sense of incompleteness: even in friendship or closeness, there remains a deeper longing that is not fully satisfied. The poem captures the delicate balance between gratitude for connection and the ache of still wanting more.
