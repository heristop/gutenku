This haiku suggests a gentle respect for the artist’s careful work. The phrase "the sweet dignity" gives the poem a calm, honorable tone, as if the artist’s effort has beauty and worth beyond fame. "The artist was diligent" emphasizes patience, discipline, and devotion to craft. The final line, "and they are still used," implies that the artist’s creations continue to serve a purpose, showing that true art can endure through time and remain meaningful in daily life. Together, the haiku celebrates skill, usefulness, and the lasting dignity of creative labor.
