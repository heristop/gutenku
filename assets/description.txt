This haiku conveys a quiet sense of waiting, loss, and the passage of time. The first line, "Still She returned not," suggests the speaker’s disappointment and continued hope that someone important will come back. The second line, "resumed his former station," implies a return to an earlier place, role, or state, but it also feels uncertain, as if life has gone back to its usual position while the desired person remains absent. The final line, "The night flies apace," highlights how quickly time passes, deepening the feeling of loneliness and impatience. Together, the lines create a mood of restrained sorrow, where absence is felt intensely even as the world moves on.
