This haiku creates a quiet, intimate mood through its soft language and restrained emotion. The phrase "though in subdued tones" suggests a hushed, careful voice, as if the speaker is speaking privately or with tenderness. "as I had the night before" hints at a continuing moment, memory, or repeated encounter, giving the poem a sense of lingering feeling and continuity. The final image, "the fair-haired beauty," introduces a striking figure who seems admired from a distance, but the speaker’s tone remains controlled rather than openly passionate. Overall, the haiku captures a delicate moment of admiration, memory, and quiet emotional tension.
