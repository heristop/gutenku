This haiku suggests a moment of quiet grief and finality. The speaker says that someone "made no answer," creating a sense of silence, distance, or emotional withdrawal. The line about "The doctor picked it up" implies a medical setting, perhaps after an illness or death, and the object being picked up may symbolize something left behind or taken away. The final line, "my last one had done," hints that this was not the first such loss, but another in a series of painful experiences. The poem is brief, but it carries a deep feeling of sorrow, resignation, and the heaviness of repeated endings.
