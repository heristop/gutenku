This haiku captures the tense atmosphere of conflict through sharp, questioning language. The phrase "where the fighting is" suggests a search for the battlefield or the center of danger, while "who demanded the watchword" hints at authority, secrecy, and discipline. The final line, "in a warlike tone," intensifies the mood, showing aggression and urgency. As a whole, the poem evokes the sound and pressure of war, where even speech carries force and suspicion.
