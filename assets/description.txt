This haiku suggests a quiet but intense moment of movement and anticipation. The line "Lawrence followed him" introduces a sense of mystery, as one person trails another, hinting at curiosity, duty, or tension. "It was a glorious night" shifts the mood toward beauty and wonder, suggesting that the setting is calm, clear, and perhaps emotionally significant. The final line, "In a few moments," creates suspense and leaves the reader expecting an important event to unfold soon. Together, the haiku blends atmosphere, action, and anticipation in a brief but vivid scene.
