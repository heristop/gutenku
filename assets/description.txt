This haiku presents a striking contrast between warmth, violence, and emotional control. The image of someone “smiling at the fire” suggests danger, pain, or emotional intensity, yet the smile implies calmness, resilience, or even defiance. The next line, “throw cold water over him,” introduces a sudden act of shock or attempted suppression, as if someone is trying to extinguish his spirit or passion. The final phrase, “composed of a stone,” deepens the meaning by portraying the person as unshaken, hard, and emotionally impenetrable. Overall, the haiku suggests a character who remains steady and composed despite pressure, hardship, or hostility.
