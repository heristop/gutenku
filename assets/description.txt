This haiku has a dry, witty tone that seems to poke fun at experts and certainty. The phrase "like all specialists" suggests people who claim authority, while "if it is as we suspect" introduces doubt rather than confidence. The final line, "have it your own way," sounds dismissive and ironic, as if the speaker is stepping back from debate and letting others believe what they want. Overall, the haiku captures skepticism, subtle sarcasm, and the limits of expertise in a few brief lines.
