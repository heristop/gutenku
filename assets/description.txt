This haiku suggests a speaker exploring the sea’s mysterious depths and discovering that it feels limitless. The phrase "tried deep-sea soundings" implies an attempt to measure or understand the unknown, while "infinite in its extent" emphasizes vastness and awe. The final line, "This wondrous sea is," leaves the thought open-ended, inviting the reader to feel the sea’s beauty, mystery, and endless possibility. Overall, the poem captures wonder before nature’s immensity.
