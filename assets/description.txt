This haiku suggests a brief moment of brightness interrupted by a return of darkness or threat. The image of the sun hints at hope, clarity, or peace, but the sudden arrival of the shadow creates a feeling of disruption and unease. The final line, “with the polished boots,” adds a human presence—perhaps someone official, powerful, or intimidating—whose polished boots symbolize authority, control, or a cold, deliberate march into the scene. The poem’s simple images create a tense contrast between light and shadow, calm and intrusion, making the moment feel both vivid and unsettling.
