This haiku feels reflective and slightly mysterious, as if the speaker is looking back on a moment of questioning that happened sometime in the past. The phrase "one day at this time" creates a sense of a specific memory, while "to inquire particulars" suggests a careful search for details or truth. The ending, "so far as it went," gives the poem a restrained, unfinished feeling, implying that the inquiry only reached a certain limit before stopping. Overall, the haiku captures the quiet, thoughtful act of trying to understand something, while also hinting that full clarity may never be possible.
