This haiku suggests a quiet, fleeting moment of movement and observation. The phrase "as he went his rounds" evokes someone making a routine journey, perhaps a worker, sailor, or caretaker moving through the day. The line "in women who came aboard" introduces a human presence that feels sudden and delicate, as if people are arriving like part of the morning’s unfolding scene. The final image, "the fresh morning air," brings a sense of renewal, cleanliness, and beginning. Overall, the haiku captures the gentle transition from night to day, blending human activity with the calm freshness of morning.
