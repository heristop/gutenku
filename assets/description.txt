This haiku suggests the quiet power of a serene presence hidden in darkness. The phrase "in whose look serene" evokes a calm, gentle gaze, while "though hid in gloomiest shade" creates a contrast between inner peace and outer gloom. The final line, "thus the silence broke," implies that this calm presence has the ability to interrupt stillness, perhaps bringing comfort, hope, or emotional release. Overall, the poem captures the beauty of serenity shining through darkness and the moment when silence is transformed by subtle but meaningful presence.
