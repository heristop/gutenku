This haiku creates a tense, eerie mood by placing a fearful storm beside the quiet presence of a loyal dog. The first line suggests danger and unease, while the second line hints that the dog stays close to its owner, offering comfort or companionship. The final line, "nor heard the dog bark," deepens the silence and mystery, making the scene feel still, almost haunting. Together, the lines suggest that even in frightening circumstances, there is calm, loyalty, and quiet endurance.
