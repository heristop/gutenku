## Design Context

### Users

Readers who enjoy classic literature and short daily rituals, mostly Western and English, French or Japanese speaking. They visit GutenKu on phone and desktop to read the haiku of the day, write their own haiku cut from public-domain books (Project Gutenberg), play GutenGuess (guess today's classic in six tries) and read the blog. Sessions are short and repeated daily; streaks and the next-puzzle countdown bring them back.

### Brand Personality

Literate, calm, quietly playful. A well-kept private library rather than an app: typewritten, ruled in ink, stamped like a library book. Delight comes from craft (a stamp pressed onto the page, a quill writing a line) never from confetti or noise.

### Aesthetic Direction

"Ex Libris" ("from the books of"). Each page is a leaf from a library book.

- Materials: paper surfaces (never pure white), sumi ink for text and 1.5px structural rules, indigo only on things you can press, one vermilion stamp ink for verdicts and the GK monogram (under 5% of a page). Pigments follow traditional Japanese values: sumi `#27221F`, indigo (ai-iro) `#264348`, vermilion (ginshu) `#BC2D29`, paper `#F5EFE3`.
- Type: JMH Typewriter is the only face. Hierarchy from scale and weight; oversized tabular numerals; negative tracking on display sizes.
- Marks: the GK rubber stamp, brush-rule section dividers, ink-drop tries, torn index-card clue slips, the ink strike for eliminated items, a ruled colophon with a "Stamped" box for results.
- Spine: a vertical page name on the left edge at desktop width, like a title on a shelf.
- The sumi-e mountains and the haiku carry the Japanese side, where it means something. No kanji or Japanese emblems as decoration: the audience is mostly Western.
- Night theme: dark sumi paper, gofun-white ink, a warmer vermilion; tuned values, never inverted.
- Icons: the in-house hand-drawn ink set (`@/core/components/icons/ink`), one stroke weight. No Lucide, no emoji as icons (Bookmoji clues are game content).
- Loading: one site-wide writing quill (`InkLoadingIndicator` + `useInkLoading`), never per-card loaders.

### Design Principles

- One focal point per view and generous space between sections (ma). The board, the poem or the article leads; everything else steps back.
- Indigo means "press me", vermilion means "verdict". Never use either as decoration; never add a second accent.
- No eyebrow or kicker labels above headings; meta goes under the title. No pills, gradients, glass blur, halo shadows or bouncy overshoot.
- Feedback is immediate and non-blocking: toasts never cover the board, errors say what happened and how to recover, a failed request is never counted as a wrong guess.
- Motion marks a change of state only: ease-out tokens (`--gk-ease-out`), 600ms or less, sound on the same frame as the stamp impact, a reduced-motion fallback for every animation.
- Accessible by default: 44px touch targets, visible indigo focus rings, 4.5:1 text contrast in both themes, one polite live announcement per game event, keyboard paths for every action.
- Tokens only: `--gk-*` in `packages/front/src/assets/css/exlibris.scss` (legacy `--gutenku-*` tokens resolve to them). Shared primitives live in `packages/front/src/core/components/ink/`.
